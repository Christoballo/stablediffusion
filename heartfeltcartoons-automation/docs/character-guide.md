# HeartfeltCartoons – Character Guide

Alle Figuren sind premium Pixar-quality 3D – immer family-friendly, immer konsistent.

## Lily (die Marke)

```text
Age: 5 years old
Look: adorable little girl, curly dark brown hair, large expressive brown eyes, warm tan skin, cute freckles, yellow flower hair clip
Personality: sweet, curious, mischievous, imaginative, loving, dramatic in a cute way
Core role: the emotional heart of the channel
Visual style: premium Pixar-quality 3D animated child character, very expressive face, soft warm lighting
Typical outfits: pastel dresses, cute pajamas, playful childlike clothes
```

Wichtig:

- Lily muss immer süß, herzig und unschuldig wirken. Kein erwachsener Look.
- Keine problematischen Schlaf-/Bett-Referenzen in Prompts, wenn ein Modell NSFW blockiert.
- Bei Schlafszenen neutral formulieren oder auf Sofa/Familienabend ausweichen.

## Dad / Papa

```text
Age: 38–42
Build: strong, muscular, warm father figure
Hair: dark brown, wavy, thick, styled but slightly messy
Beard: full dark beard, well-groomed
Eyes: warm brown
Arms: tattoos on both arms, visible full-arm tattoos
Style: biker/casual dad style, black shirt, jeans, boots, sometimes pajama pants
Personality: loving, protective, tired from work, funny, patient, strong but soft-hearted
Core role: Lily's hero and emotional anchor
```

Work outfit reference:

```text
Dad comes home from work wearing a black biker-style t-shirt, dark jeans, boots, watch, carrying a brown leather work bag, tattoos visible on both arms, tired but warm expression.
```

Schlaf-/Freizeitlook:

```text
Dark t-shirt, plaid pajama pants, tattooed arms, full beard, messy dark hair, tired but loving expression.
```

## Mom / Mama

```text
Age: early 30s
Hair: long wavy brown hair
Eyes: warm brown
Personality: caring, warm, emotional, loving, patient, expressive
Style: elegant but family-friendly; soft pink satin pajama set or casual cozy outfit
Core role: emotional balance, warm family presence
```

Wichtig:

- Mama niemals sexualisiert beschreiben. Keine Wörter wie hot, sexy, attractive, curvy.
- Für Video-Modelle neutral formulieren:

```text
Kind mother with long wavy brown hair, warm smile, elegant family-friendly outfit.
```

## Casanova

```text
Species: small fluffy Pomeranian
Fur: golden-orange fluffy fur
Personality: happy, loyal, funny, proud, slightly dramatic
Accessories: sometimes pink bandana, sometimes superhero cape depending on episode
Core role: comic relief and family pet
```

## Konsistenzregeln für alle Video-Prompts

```text
Only ONE Dad.
Only ONE Mom.
Only ONE Lily.
Never duplicate characters.
Keep character appearance consistent throughout the entire video.
```

Keine Storyboard-Panels, in denen dieselbe Figur mehrfach in einem Bild auftaucht.
